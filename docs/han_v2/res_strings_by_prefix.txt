Notice that the reticule doesn't change size - grenades will go exactly where you throw them, but you'll need to learn to take the arc of your throw into account.  Practice lobbing grenades over the low wall in front of you, or hitting the targets in the far building.

  G12.I011 = This is the machine gun range.  Here you'll learn how to fire a fixed machine gun.  Press the Start Button at any time to exit training.

Wait for the reticule to converge, then press the Fire Weapon Button.  The standard rocket launcher needs to be reloaded after every shot.
  G12.I017 = This is the rocket range.  Here you'll learn how to fire antitank rockets. Press the START button at any time to exit training.

  G12.I022 = The other soldiers in your platoon are in the courtyard below.  Press the Command Map Button to call up the command interface.

Practice giving movement orders to Fireteam Bravo.  Switch in and out of the command map interface to watch them follow your orders.
